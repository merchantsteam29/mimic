● REC  Mimic · voice recording

Record the tapes

  1. Turn on your microphone below, and pick who you're recording.
  2. Press ● Record, say the big words, press ■ Stop. The grey how notes are directions: don't say them.
  3. Not happy? Press Redo. Your takes are kept in this browser until you download them.
  4. When you're done, press Download this part at the bottom and send the zip over.

Tip: Space starts and stops recording the highlighted line, and it moves on to your next line by itself.

Use headphones or a quiet room. No effects: the game adds the tape crackle.
