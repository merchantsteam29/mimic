How to play

Something is in here with you. It doesn't have a voice of its own, so it records yours.

  • Every time you talk, it keeps a recording. Later it plays your own words back from the dark to lure you in.
  • It can hear you. Talking, shouting and sprinting all bring it to you. Watch the mic bar: when it turns red, it heard you.
  • Every run has 2–3 jobs before you can restore the power: find fuses for the panel, repair generators (hit the skill checks or they bang), smash its tapes (each one erases some of the voices it stole), and sometimes find a keycard for a locked room. Then pull the power lever and get out.
  • Classes (all free): Brute has 2 flares that stun it. Runner is fast. Medic can save one person from a catch. Tracker has a motion tracker.
  • If it follows you to your hiding spot, it stops at the door and listens. Keep your heart steady (Space on the beat) and stay silent.
  • Hide in lockers and wardrobes. It can't see you there, but it can still hear you.
  • The map is different every time: a research facility or an old house.
  • With friends (up to 4): voice chat is proximity-based. If a friend goes quiet, they might just be lost. Or it might be using their voice now.
