## Title

Mimic

## Short description (one line, under 100 characters)

It learns your voice. Then it uses it to call your friends into the dark.

## Long description

**Mimic** is a first-person co-op horror game you play in your browser, alone or with up to three friends.

Something is loose in the building. It listens. Everything you say, it records, and later it plays your voice back to
lure people to it: a friend calling for help from a room they couldn't be in, a sentence you never said, your own
scream, far away.

Get the power back on and get out. Find fuses, fix generators, smash the tapes it learned you from. Hide in lockers,
under beds, in vents. Keep your flashlight on and it sees you from across the building; turn it off and you can't see
it coming.

**Play with friends** (up to 4, proximity voice chat):
- When someone is caught, their mic just cuts out. No message. You'll think they wandered off.
- Then it starts using their voice.
- The dead can haunt the living: flicker lights, knock, whisper. You can't tell it from the real thing.

**What's in there:**
- 12 places, a new layout every time: a research facility, an old house, a hospital, a mall, a school at night, a
  hotel, a church, an asylum, the woods, a subway station where the trains still run, an abandoned fairground, and an old mine.
- More than the mimic: the skinwalker that wears a living friend, a blind listener, a stalker that only moves when
  nobody's looking, a shadow that dies in the light, your own double, a phone that rings with the voices of the taken,
  a huge singing Mother, a clown that looks just like the statues, and a patient that gets up when you run.
- Modes: Normal, Imposter voices (one more of you than there should be), a traitor story, Hunt (one of you plays the mimic), Versus, Night Shift (last until 6 a.m.), Hide and
  seek, Escort (one lantern for everyone).
- Two solo stories, voiced: *Ellie* (8 chapters) and *The Operator* (5 chapters).
- 8 classes, all free from the start.
- The kill-cam: see how it got you. Save the jumpscare as a video. Share the voice that fooled you.

Free. No accounts, no shop, nothing to unlock.

**Microphone:** optional, but it's the whole point. Your recordings stay in your browser. Online, they only go to the
players in your room, peer to peer, and they're gone when you close the tab.

**Controls:** mouse and keyboard, controller, or touch. Works on desktop and phones. Headphones recommended.

## Content notes

Jumpscares, flashing lights (Settings > Fewer flashes turns them down), disturbing imagery, the player's own voice used
against them. No gore. Suggested age 13+.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / look | WASD / mouse | Left / right stick |
| Use, pick up, hide | E | A |
| Flashlight | F | X |
| Sprint / crouch | Shift / C | LT / B |
| Class ability | G | Y |
| Jobs | Tab | View |
| Walkie-talkie (online) | B (hold) | D-pad down |

All keys can be rebound in Settings > Controls.
